Rules

  • A price is never alone. “$45” and “$45 per guest, 10 minimum” are different offers, and the first one is the one that turns into an argument on the phone. The unit is part of the price, set in Montserrat 600 at about a third of the price's size.
  • Playfair only above ~20px. It is a high-contrast display serif — its thin strokes disappear at body sizes on a phone screen and in print. Anything a parent reads a sentence of is Montserrat.
  • One Playfair weight per document (700). Mixing 500 and 700 reads as two brands.
  • Letter-spacing belongs to the eyebrow and nothing else. Tracked-out uppercase is the accent texture; using it on a heading as well spends the effect twice.
  • Line length caps at 68 characters. A menu is scanned, not read, and a full-width paragraph across a letter page is the single fastest way to make a parent skip the thing you most wanted them to see.
  • Fall back honestly. font-family:'Playfair Display',Georgia,serif and Montserrat,system-ui,sans-serif. A menu opened offline or printed from a locked-down school computer must still look deliberate.
