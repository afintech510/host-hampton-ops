Colour

Five tokens. A party menu is a document a parent reads on a phone in a school pickup line, so contrast is not decoration — it is whether the price is legible.

Ivory
#F7F2E8 · hampton-ivory
The page. Every menu sits on this, never on white — white is for cards raised off it.
Navy
#2F343B · hampton-navy
All body text and headings. Also the header bar. Despite the name it is a warm dark grey.
Gold
#C7A36B · hampton-pink
The accent, and the only colour that may mark something as chosen: the "Most Booked" package, a price that matters.
Dusty blue
#8FA8BF · hampton-blue
Secondary fills and quiet section bands. Never text on ivory — it fails contrast.
Silver
#AEB6C2 · hampton-mauve
Rules, dividers, disabled states. Structure you should not notice.

Rules

  • Gold is a scarce resource. One gold thing per screenful. Two "Most Booked" badges is the same as none — the point of the accent is that the eye lands somewhere specific, and a parent skimming on a phone gets one landing.
  • Text is navy on ivory or navy on white. That is the whole text system. Measured: navy on ivory is 11.2:1, navy on white 12.5:1. Blue (2.21:1) and silver (1.83:1) on ivory both fail AA body text, which needs 4.5:1. They are fills, not ink.
  • Gold is never text. Gold on ivory measures 2.12:1 — it fails AA body text (4.5:1) and AA large text (3:1), so there is no size at which it becomes acceptable. This is the rule most likely to be broken by instinct, because a gold price looks expensive and premium; it is also unreadable to a lot of people on a phone in daylight. Gold is a fill, a rule, or a badge background — put navy on gold (5.3:1, passes AA) when you need the accent to carry a word. Never white on gold (2.36:1).
  • Safe ink-on-fill pairs, all measured: navy on gold 5.3:1 · navy on blue 5.09:1 · white on navy 12.54:1. Those three plus navy-on-ivory/white are the complete set.
  • Never introduce a sixth colour for a theme. A Slime Party menu and a Spa Party menu are the same brand; the theme lives in the photography and the copy, not in a green palette. The one exception is a fundraiser storefront, which carries the school's colours on purpose — see esm-sharks.
  • Print check. Ivory backgrounds vanish if "Background graphics" is off in the print dialog, so a menu must still parse in black on white. Do not encode meaning in a background fill alone — pair it with a rule, a weight change or a label.

Naming trap: the Tailwind tokens are misleading and have been since they were written — hampton-pink is gold and hampton-navy is grey. They are not renamed here because the class names are live across the whole site. Read the hex, not the name.
