Colour + patch on every item

Three ways a parent could pick both, on a phone, without us shooting twelve new product photos. Everything below is a sketch for a decision — nothing is built yet.

Two things I need from you before I build

1. Do loose patches stay a product? Today a patch is also sold on its own — $8, or 3 for $20. If a patch is also a required choice on every hat, tote and pouch, then a line reading "Circle Patch × 2" in the order book is ambiguous: is that two loose patches in an envelope, or the decoration on two hats? Keep them, drop them, or rename them ("Patch — on its own")?
2. Does the patch change the price? Circle and script are different sizes and stitch counts. Same $25 hat either way, or does one cost more?

And one thing to look at

The two patches are not the same blue. The circle crest is the deep navy the whole site and the flyer are built on. The script is a noticeably brighter royal. Side by side in a picker, a parent will see it — so it's worth knowing whether that's the real thread colour or just how the mockup was rendered.

Not a blocker either way — but if they're meant to match, it's much cheaper to fix with the embroiderer now than after a hundred are stitched.

Nothing is remembered behind the scenes — what you see in the boxes is exactly what gets made. The colour buttons are relabelled "Show navy / Show silver" so it's clear they only change the picture.

For

  • No hidden state. Every combination is visible with its own number
  • Any mix at all — one circle hat and one script hat together
  • The item name writes itself, so the order book and the emails can't be vague
  • Same mental model as today, just one more row

Against

  • Four boxes per card instead of two — a longer page on a phone
  • Slightly more to take in at a glance

Either way

The mascot patch comes off the page. The shark-on-its-own design isn't one of the two you picked, so its card, its photo and its tile on the printed flyer all go, and the flyer changes from "3 designs" to "2 designs".

Every item gets a longer name. "Navy Trucker Hat" no longer says what was bought, so it becomes "Navy Trucker Hat — Circle Patch" everywhere a human reads it: the order book, the CSV, your confirmation email and the parent's.
