# MECHANICS — read before scheduling

### Email: the 300/day cap is real

Brevo free is **300 emails/day** against **968 sendable contacts**. A full send
is **four consecutive days**, not one. Migration 052 put the audience on the
campaign row and the lists are 4/5/6/7. Plan the announcement to *start* four
days before you want everyone to have it.

Also: the Brevo lists *index* endpoint reports 0 subscribers and is lying — read
the single-list endpoint if you want a real count.

### Email: 253 of your people aren't in Brevo at all

`contactSync` used `PUT /v3/contacts/{email}`, which 404s on an address Brevo has
never seen, so it never *created* anyone — **253 contacts exist in our database
and not in the marketing list.** If you want them on this send they need pushing
across first. That's tracked as needs-Adam 32.

### SMS: two problems, one of them blocking

1. **The Twilio number's inbound webhook points at a different project**, so
   **`STOP` never reaches our database.** Every draft above says "Reply STOP to
   opt out" and right now that promise is not kept on our side. Before any
   marketing SMS goes out, either fix the webhook or confirm the carrier-level
   STOP is handling it — sending bulk marketing texts that silently swallow opt-outs
   is the one item here I'd genuinely hold the send for.
2. **Quiet hours.** TCPA is 8am–9pm *in the recipient's local time*, and the box
   runs **UTC** — this is exactly the bug that scheduled every reminder 4–5 hours
   early (migration 044 / link 46). If you schedule the 8:30am text, verify the
   timezone it actually fires in. Send by hand if in doubt.

### Nothing is scheduled to send itself

`process-sequences` stopped on 2026-08-16 and **must not be rescheduled without
Adam** — 44 enrollments are frozen behind it. The outbound send path has nothing
scheduled at all. Treat every send above as **manual**.

### Retargeting

A separate session is planning retargeting ads. Market RSVPers and
`/christmas-market` visitors are the obvious first audience — but there's no Meta
Pixel on the site today, so that audience only starts filling once one is
installed. If retargeting matters for this market, the pixel needs to go up
**before** the Nov 10 email, not after.
