1. Payment Terms & Deposits

This Agreement uses two separate deposits, which serve different purposes:

  • Reservation Deposit (25%, non-refundable): Due at booking to secure your date. It is applied toward your Total Rental and is not refundable, including if you cancel.
  • Security Deposit (refundable hold): A refundable authorization hold placed on the Client's payment card on or about the day of the event. It is a hold, not a charge; it auto-releases within approximately 7 days if no issues are found. It may be captured (charged), in whole or part, to cover cleaning, repairs, replacement of damaged items, overtime, lost-property recovery, or policy violations.

Balance: The remaining balance (Total Rental minus Reservation Deposit) is due 7 days before the event. Add-on quantities (e.g., final guest count for per-person items) lock 7 days before the event; some décor and food items may lock earlier.

Payments are non-transferable to other dates unless approved in writing by Host Hampton. Late or missed payment may result in cancellation of the reservation, in which case the Reservation Deposit is forfeited.

Card Authorization. By signing, the Client authorizes Host Hampton to charge the payment card on file for: (a) the balance due if not otherwise paid by the due date; (b) overtime as described in Section 4; and (c) damages, excessive cleaning, missing items, or policy violations as described in Sections 5–6, up to and including capture of the Security Deposit hold and any amount in excess that is documented.

2. What's Included

  • Private use of the studio during your reserved rental time.
  • Standard tables and chairs (seating up to 65; standing room up to 85).
  • Wi-Fi, Bluetooth sound, prep area, and restroom access.
  • Basic cleanup guidelines provided at check-in (trash bag locations, wiping surfaces, etc.).

3. What's Not Included (Unless Added)

  • Staff-led activities, themed décor setup, balloons, or a party host.
  • Food, desserts, beverages, paper goods, candles, utensils, or serving supplies.
  • Photographer, DJ, specialty lighting, or dedicated tech support.
  • Garbage removal (unless a clean-up/garbage add-on is purchased — otherwise all garbage must be removed).

4. Setup, Breakdown & Overtime

Your rental time must include all setup and breakdown. Access before your start time is not guaranteed and may be denied if another event is in progress. Exceeding your end time impacts other bookings.

  • Overtime: charged in 15-minute increments at $25 per 15 minutes, authorized against the card on file.
  • Check-in / out: the Client is responsible for a walk-through at the start and end of the rental.
  • The Client must not exceed the stated occupancy limits at any time.

5. Security Deposit, Damage & Cleaning

The Security Deposit (Section 1) may be applied to cleaning, repairs, replacement of damaged items, overtime, or policy violations. If no issues are found, the hold is released within approximately 7 days. The Client is responsible for damage caused by their guests, vendors, or children.

  • Do not move large furniture without approval.
  • No adhesives that remove paint or leave residue (including certain balloon tapes).
  • Bag all trash and remove it from the studio; remove all personal items, décor, food, and favors.
  • Wipe down obvious spills and food messes; leave floors reasonably free of debris.

If excessive cleaning is required, cleaning fees may be charged.

6. Food, Alcohol & Candles

  • Outside food: allowed unless otherwise noted for your booking.
  • Alcohol: only if explicitly approved in writing and compliant with local laws; no glass bottles unless approved. Host Hampton may require a licensed/insured bartender. The Client is solely responsible for the lawful, responsible service of alcohol and assumes all related liability.
  • Candles: permitted for cake only; no open-flame décor. LED candles recommended.

7. Safety, Supervision & Conduct

  • Children must be supervised at all times by the Client and/or designated adults.
  • No running outside the studio, climbing on furniture, or unsafe behavior.
  • No weapons, illegal substances, or smoking/vaping on the premises.
  • Host Hampton may end an event for unsafe or disruptive behavior; refunds are not guaranteed.

8. Cancellations, Reschedules & Weather

  • Cancellation: the 25% Reservation Deposit is non-refundable. If the Client cancels more than 30 days before the event, amounts paid above the deposit are refunded; if within 30 days, 50% of the balance paid is refundable; if within 7 days, no amounts paid are refundable.
  • Reschedule: one reschedule may be accommodated when requested at least 14 days in advance, subject to availability; date changes are not guaranteed.
  • Severe weather / emergencies: Host Hampton will work with you on the next best option (such as a reschedule credit) when feasible.

9. Liability Waiver & Indemnification

The Client assumes responsibility for all guests and acknowledges that participation in events carries inherent risks. To the fullest extent permitted by law, the Client releases, holds harmless, and agrees to indemnify and defend Host Hampton, its owners, staff, and affiliates from any claims, damages, losses, or expenses (including reasonable attorneys' fees) related to injury, loss, or damage arising from the Client's event, guests, or vendors — except to the extent caused by Host Hampton's gross negligence or willful misconduct.

10. Vendor Requirements

  • All vendors (balloons, catering, entertainment, photographers, etc.) must be disclosed in advance.
  • Vendors must operate within your reserved time window and follow studio rules.
  • Host Hampton may require proof of insurance for certain vendors or activities.

11. Photography & Marketing

Host Hampton may photograph or video the studio and event setup for marketing and social media. The Client may opt out by notifying Host Hampton in writing before the event. The Client is responsible for obtaining any consent needed from their own guests.
