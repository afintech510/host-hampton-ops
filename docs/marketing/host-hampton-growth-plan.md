# Host Hampton — SEO & Growth Review + 90-Day Action Plan

_Working strategy from the sessions that built the mobile-craft-party + craft-page SEO surface. The
[Fable deep-review prompt](./fable-deep-seo-review-prompt.md) will pressure-test and extend this with
live search/competitor data. This is the actionable baseline._

Date: 2026-09-04 · GBP: 25 ratings, 5.0 ★

---

## 1. Where we stand (review)

**Strengths**
- **Two-venue model (studio OR mobile)** — a genuine differentiator; most LI competitors are one or
  the other. Now messaged across the new pages.
- **5.0 from 25 reviews** — strong trust signal, now in site schema (AggregateRating) for rich results.
- **Flexibility** — almost any theme, any reasonable craft. Underused in content today.
- **New SEO footprint** — mobile hub + 26 town pages + 9 craft/theme pages + real sitemap + schema.

**Gaps (what this plan attacks)**
1. **Flexibility isn't captured in search.** We rank for the themes we named; we're invisible for the
   long tail of themes/crafts people actually search that we *can* do.
2. **Top-of-funnel content is thin.** No "ideas/how-to/cost" content to catch early-stage planners.
3. **Seasonal demand isn't pre-published.** Communion (spring), beach-house summer, Halloween, winter
   break, and shower season all need pages live 60–90 days ahead.
4. **B2B/community whitespace** (corporate activations, schools/PTA, scouts, camps, libraries, adult
   craft-and-sip) is barely addressed.
5. **Conversion**: price transparency + the two-venue choice could be clearer on money pages.

---

## 2. Strategy (the through-line)

**Own "any theme, any craft, your place or ours, anywhere on Long Island."** Compete on *coverage +
flexibility + trust*, not price. Three fronts:

- **A. Capture existing demand** — dedicated pages for high-intent themes/crafts/towns we can win, plus
  GBP optimization for "near me."
- **B. Create demand** — idea/how-to/cost content that catches planners early and funnels to quotes.
- **C. Expand the buyer** — B2B activations, schools/scouts/camps, community & adult programs, repeat/
  referral. Different sales cycles, same crew and stations.

**Guardrail:** no doorway pages. New pages must be genuinely distinct; combinations (theme×town) are
covered by internal linking, not thin duplicates. Prune anything with zero impressions at 90 days.

## 3. 90-Day Action Plan (prioritized)

### Phase 1 — Now → 3 weeks (foundation & fast wins)
1. **GBP optimization** (biggest "near me" lever, low effort): confirm primary + secondary categories
   (Children's party service, Party planner, Event venue, Permanent jewelry if allowed), fill the
   Services list to mirror site pages, set the service-area radius, add fresh photos per craft/theme,
   post weekly, and answer/seed Q&A. `[owner action]`
2. **Ship the built work** — commit + deploy the mobile/craft/town pages, review schema (25/5.0), and
   homepage band. Submit `/sitemap.xml` in Search Console; request indexing on the hub + top pages.
3. **Real photos** for the 3 pages using placeholders (mermaid, squishy, balloon-dog drip art). Swap in.
4. **A "themes we can do" page** (`/party-themes` or expand `/party-packages`) that explicitly says
   *we do custom + any theme*, with a broad, keyword-rich (but honest) theme list. This is how the
   flexibility becomes searchable without one-thin-page-per-theme.

### Phase 2 — Weeks 3–7 (capture high-intent demand)
5. **2–4 more craft/theme pages** where demand is real and we're absent — likely candidates to validate
   with data first: **tie-dye party**, **paint & sip / kids paint party**, **sensory / quiet party**,
   **teen/tween party**, **science/STEM party**, **holiday craft party**. (Data-gated — build the ones
   the Fable review confirms.)
6. **Corporate / brand activation page** beyond totes — a `/activations` or `/corporate-events` hub
   (team-building, pop-ups, employee family days) linking `/canvas-tote-activation` and `/trucker-hat-bar`.
7. **Schools / scouts / camps page** — field-trip, PTA, troop-badge, and after-school craft programs.
8. **Internal-linking pass** — ensure every money page cross-links relevant craft + town pages, and the
   two-venue message ("studio OR your house") is on all of them.

### Phase 3 — Weeks 7–13 (create demand + seasonal)
9. **Top-of-funnel content** (3–5 posts): "at-home craft party ideas for kids on Long Island,"
   "how much does a kids birthday party cost in the Hamptons," "best crafts by age," "mobile vs. studio
   party — which is right for you." Each links down into money pages.
10. **Seasonal pages live ahead of demand:** communion season (build by Jan for spring), summer
    beach-house parties (build by Apr), Halloween/fall craft (build by Aug), winter-break camps.
11. **Reviews flywheel** — a simple post-party ask that requests a Google review mentioning the *town*
    and *at-home/studio*; surface a few more real reviews on-site to strengthen schema.
12. **Measure & prune** — Search Console review of town/craft pages; kill zero-impression pages, deepen
    the ones getting clicks (add photos, testimonials, more local detail).
