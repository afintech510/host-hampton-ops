## 2. The verdict on $750 for 10 *(original analysis — superseded by 1b)*

**$750 is defensible — but it lands at the very top of the market, and it is not a "starting at"
number.** Two things are true at once:

**It's justified.** At $75/child you are level with Emily's trailer and The Slime Machine's real
all-in cost. And you are not selling the same thing they are: those are **single-craft, 60–90
minute** experiences. Your mobile party is **multi-station, fully hosted, decor included, setup and
cleanup handled, with a keepsake per child.** It is also *below* your own studio rate for the same
headcount (glow party is $950 for 10), which it must be or you cannibalize the studio.

**But it's exposed as a headline.** A parent comparison-shopping sees **"$750 for 10"** next to
**"$499 for 12, birthday child free, no travel fee"** and stops reading. The plan's whole point in
B-1 was to publish an anchor that *wins* the comparison — a number that loses it is worse than no
number. "Starting at" specifically signals *this is my cheapest option*, which $750 is not.

### Recommendation: tier it, and let the entry tier be the anchor

| Tier | What it is | Suggested price |
|---|---|---|
| **Entry** — "from $X" | 1 craft station, ~60–90 min, hosted, setup + cleanup | **$450–$550 for 10** |
| **Signature** | 2–3 craft stations, ~2 hrs, decor, hosted | **$750 for 10** ← your number |
| **Premium** | 4+ stations / add-ons / larger groups | $950+ |

Publish **"Mobile craft parties from $[entry] — 10 kids, we bring everything"** and show $750 as the
popular middle tier. You capture the price-shopper *and* protect the premium position, instead of
losing the click before you can explain the value. Classic good/better/best — and the middle tier is
what most people pick.

**If you only want one number**, $750/10 still works, but never label it "starting at." Label it for
what it is and put the inclusions right next to it: *"$750 — 10 kids, 3 craft stations, 2 hours,
fully hosted, setup & cleanup, a keepsake each."* Per-child value has to be visible at a glance.

## 3. Two edges to press regardless of the number

1. **No East End travel surcharge.** The Slime Machine charges outside Nassau; Paint Party LI charges
   **+$75 east of Riverhead**. You are *based* in Speonk — the Hamptons and both forks are your home
   turf. This is already live on `/paint-party` and should be on every mobile page and the price block.
   (Note: Emily's also advertises zero travel fees, so this beats some rivals, not all.)
2. **All-in, honest pricing.** The Slime Machine adds tax **plus a 20% mandatory gratuity** — a $675
   headline is really ~$810. Emily's markets hard on "no hidden fees, no mandatory gratuity." If your
   number is all-in, **say so explicitly next to the price.** At the premium end, that is a real
   conversion lever and it costs nothing.
